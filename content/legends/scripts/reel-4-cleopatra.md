## Cast

### Guy in sunglasses

A man in his thirties in a shiny black bomber jacket, dark sunglasses at night, overconfident smirk.

### Company rep

A woman in her forties in a navy blazer, calm and prepared, holding a slim folder of registration papers and a phone.

### Delivery men

Two delivery men in grey coveralls carrying a long rolled patterned rug on their shoulders, trying very hard to look casual.

## Sets

### HQ entrance

A sleek glass-and-black-steel building entrance at night. Wet pavement reflecting warm coral light from inside, a velvet rope in front of the door.

### The lobby

A large dark lobby inside the same building, polished black floor, a wall-sized screen.

## Shots

### Shot 1 · 6s

- **Visual:** Night. Sleek HQ entrance, Cleopatra at the door with a tablet.
- **Camera:** Wide, low angle, then a slow push-in to medium on Cleopatra.
- **Action:** Cleopatra stands at the door beside the velvet rope, tablet in hand, and looks up at the camera, unimpressed.
- **Line:** VO: "Cleopatra once got into a palace rolled up in a rug." Cleopatra: "So I know every way in."
- **Sound:** Night city ambience, light traffic on wet pavement.

### Shot 2 · 4s

- **Visual:** Guy in sunglasses: "I need a million home IPs. No questions." She holds out her hand. He gives her a clearly fake ID.
- **Camera:** Over her shoulder onto the guy, then an insert on the ID in her hand.

- **Line:** Guy in sunglasses: "I need a million home IPs. No questions." Cleopatra: "Next."
- **Sound:** Night ambience, the flick of a plastic card.

- **Visual:** Liveness check on her tablet: the camera shows a rubber mask.

### Shot 4 · 4s

- **Visual:** A company rep hands over registration papers; the domain and card name match. Quick call about the use case. She nods him in.
- **Camera:** Medium two-shot at the door, eye level, static.
- **Action:** The rep hands over registration papers. Cleopatra checks them against her tablet, where the domain and card name match, holds a brief phone call, then nods and unhooks the velvet rope.
- **Screen:** Her tablet showing the company's domain and the card name, both marked as a match.
- **Line:** "Welcome."
- **Sound:** Paper rustle, the rope clip unhooking.

### Shot 5 · 6s

- **Visual:** Two delivery men carry in a rolled rug. She taps it with her heel. A muffled voice: "Nobody's in here."
- **Camera:** Wide as the delivery men arrive, then a low-angle close-up on her heel tapping the rug.
- **Action:** Two delivery men carry a rolled rug toward the door. She steps in front of them and taps the rug with her heel. A muffled voice answers from inside. She doesn't move.
- **Line:** Voice from the rug, muffled: "Nobody's in here." Cleopatra: "Nobody rolls in unannounced."
- **Sound:** Heel tapping on the rolled rug, the muffled voice.

### Shot 6 · 4s

- **Screen:** The lobby screen reads "69% of new accounts rejected. Published."
- **Line:** "We don't hide the numbers."
- **Sound:** A low electrical hum as the screen lights up, her heels on the polished floor.
