Compliance & Ethical Sourcing Overview

How Massive sources, governs, and safeguards its residential network

Massive operates a 100% ethically-sourced residential proxy network. As scrutiny of the proxy and web-data industry increases, we want our customers, partners, and regulators to understand exactly how our network is built and governed. Consent and integrity are not features we bolted on; they are the foundation the company was built on in 2018. This overview summarizes the policies and safeguards that keep the Massive network clean, lawful, and defensible. Full documentation is available at docs.joinmassive.com.

100% Consent & Opt-In

Every IP in the Massive network belongs to a real person who has explicitly opted in to share a portion of their idle bandwidth. This is the single most important fact about how we operate.

  • Informed, unambiguous consent. Users are clearly told what they share and what they receive in return (for example, ad-free or premium app features) before any traffic is routed.
  • Opt-in and off by default. Participation is strictly voluntary, disabled until the user agrees, and users can opt out at any time from within the host application.
  • Transparent monetization origin. The model is a consensual alternative to ads and paywalls: users exchange unused bandwidth for value.
  • No deceptive sourcing, ever. We do not, and do not permit partners to, obtain network access through silent installs, bundled malware, rooted-device harvesting, or any deceptive means.
SDK Distribution Guardrails

Network supply reaches us only through the Massive SDK, and SDK distribution is tightly controlled at every step. Our practices are documented in the SDK Privacy Policy.

  • Partner vetting (KYC). Before any partner distributes the SDK, we verify their legal identity, their application, how they acquire users, and their consent implementation.
  • Mandatory, explicit consent flow. Partners must present a clear opt-in and disclosure. The SDK stays disabled until the user affirmatively agrees, and an opt-out is always available.
  • Least-privilege by design. The SDK runs in the background on idle time and unmetered connections only, with hard per-device request and bandwidth limits, domain blocklists, and automatic removal of low-quality or misbehaving nodes.
  • Strict data isolation. The SDK has no access to user files, credentials, cookies, browsing history, or application authentication data.
  • Continuous oversight. We monitor partner traffic continuously and reserve the right to suspend or terminate any partner that violates our terms. Distribution is limited to adult, consenting users.

Network Integrity & Abuse Prevention
  • Prohibited-use policy. Illegal activity, fraud, malware distribution, CSAM, and other abuse are strictly forbidden and are grounds for immediate termination.
  • Protocol and port controls. High-risk ports and protocols are blocked at the gateway, and sensitive protocols such as SMTP are restricted.
  • Customer KYC and monitoring. We vet the businesses that use our network and watch for anomalous or abusive patterns in real time.
  • End-to-end audit trail. Every request can be traced from the consenting source device through to its destination, supporting accountability and lawful cooperation.
