Shot as a fake ambassador press conference, cutting to one quick beat from each reel. 60 to 90 seconds.

## Cast

### Reporter

A reporter in her thirties in the front row, smart casual, press badge on a lanyard, notebook in hand.

### Press pack

Rows of reporters and photographers with cameras and phones raised.

## Sets

### Press room

A press conference room: a black podium with a bank of microphones, the Massive logo on the dark wall behind it, rows of chairs, photographers along the sides, coral accent light washing the back wall.
