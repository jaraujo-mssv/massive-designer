The team's studio portraits: the eight photographed on the day, and the teammates who missed it, generated to match. One vertical 3:4 photo each, in the black Massive T-shirt against white.

Each generated item has a prompt. "Copy prompt" in the Content tab adds the teammate's description, the photo style below and the images to attach: their own photo, the two style references and the T-shirt. `npm run content:photos -- team-shoot --who="Name"` generates one.

## Photo style

A studio team portrait from Massive's team photoshoot, against a seamless white backdrop: bright pure white right behind the person and across most of the frame, almost blown out, falling off to only the faintest light grey in the top corners, with no visible floor line or horizon. Never a grey or off-white backdrop. Bright, high-key light: a large soft key from the front, slightly to one side, with even fill, so there are only soft shadows under the jaw, along the arms and in the T-shirt's folds. Full-frame camera, around a 50mm lens at f/5.6, from chest height: everything sharp from the face to the hands.

Framed vertically with generous headroom: the top of the head about a fifth of the way down the frame, the person centred, cropped at mid-thigh when standing and around the knees or shins when seated on the stool. Everyone wears the same fitted black Massive T-shirt with a round crew neck, never a V-neck, whatever top they wear in their own photo, with their own everyday trousers, and often a wristwatch. Seated poses are on a black adjustable industrial stool with a screw column and a three-legged base.

Natural, relaxed, confident expressions, looking into the lens. True-to-life skin tones, crisp detail, a clean neutral grade with a touch of warmth, no grain, vignette or filters. Photorealistic: a real person, photographed for real.
