## Cast

Outcast, Spark Tray and BizTray are made-up partner apps, played as people. Confirm none is a real partner before shooting.

### Outcast

A woman in her thirties in a grey blazer and black turtleneck, arms crossed, cool and unreadable. Name tag: "Outcast".

### Spark Tray

A young man in his twenties in a mustard-yellow hoodie, bright-eyed and eager, sitting up very straight. Name tag: "Spark Tray".

### BizTray

A man in his forties in a slightly too-shiny grey suit, slicked-back hair, a smile that's a little too wide, fidgeting with a tablet. Name tag: "BizTray".

## Sets

### Caesar's office

A glass-walled corner office high above the city on an overcast day: dark wood, charcoal walls, a clean empty desk. A laurel wreath hangs framed on the wall like an award.

### The boardroom

A long black boardroom table behind glass walls. A single pendant light hangs above each seat. Coral accent light along the far wall.

## Shots

### Shot 1 · 4s

- **Visual:** Glass office, laurel wreath framed on the wall. Caesar stares out the window.
- **Camera:** Medium, from behind him, slow arc around to his profile, the framed laurel wreath visible on the wall.
- **Action:** Caesar stands at the window with his hands clasped behind his back, staring out, then turns his head slightly, suspicious.
- **Line:** VO: "Julius Caesar has trust issues."
- **Sound:** Muffled city below, a low air-conditioning hum.

### Shot 2 · 6s

- **Visual:** Boardroom. Three partner apps sit at the table as people with name tags: Outcast, Spark Tray, BizTray.
- **Camera:** Wide, symmetrical, down the length of the table from Caesar's seat at the head, static.
- **Action:** Outcast, Spark Tray and BizTray sit along the table with name tags. Caesar sets down his coffee cup and looks at each of them in turn.
- **Line:** "I trusted my partners once. Twenty-three stab wounds."
- **Sound:** Cup set down on the table, a nervous cough from Spark Tray.

### Shot 3 · 4s

- **Visual:** He reads a partner's consent screen on a tablet, red pen in hand, before signing off.
- **Camera:** Close-up over his shoulder on the tablet, then a medium close-up of his face.
- **Action:** He reads a partner's consent screen on a tablet, tapping a red pen against his lip, circles one line, then signs off.
- **Screen:** A partner app's consent screen on a tablet, with one line circled in red.
- **Line:** "I came. I saw. I reviewed the consent text."
- **Sound:** Pen tapping, the scratch of a signature.

### Shot 4 · 6s

- **Visual:** Live dashboard pings. BizTray's screen now shows a pre-ticked box. BizTray slowly slides the tablet under a folder.
- **Camera:** Insert on the dashboard, then a medium shot of BizTray, then a close-up of Caesar still reading, not looking up.
- **Action:** The partner dashboard pings. BizTray's consent screen now shows a pre-ticked box. BizTray glances around and slowly slides his tablet under a folder. Caesar keeps reading.
- **Screen:** A partner dashboard with an alert on BizTray's row. BizTray's consent screen shows a pre-ticked box.
- **Line:** Caesar, without looking up: "I saw that."
- **Sound:** A dashboard ping, a tablet sliding under paper.

### Shot 5 · 4s

- **Visual:** Caesar taps one switch on his phone. BizTray's chair rolls away from the table, the light over his seat goes dark. On-screen: "Suspended. Traffic stops."
- **Camera:** Insert on the phone switch, then wide down the table, static.
- **Action:** Caesar taps one switch on his phone. BizTray's chair rolls slowly back from the table on its own, and the pendant light over his seat goes dark.
- **Screen:** A partner switch on his phone. After the tap it reads "Suspended. Traffic stops."
- **Line:** "Et tu, BizTray?"
- **Sound:** Switch click, chair wheels rolling, the light clunking off.

### Shot 6 · 8s

- **Visual:** End card, Caesar adjusting his cufflinks.
- **Camera:** Medium close-up, static, then the graphic end card.
- **Action:** Caesar adjusts his cufflinks, unbothered, then the end card takes over.
- **Line:** "Trust your partners. Check them anyway." VO: "He earned it, then learned to check his partners. So do we. Earned trust beats paid fame. Massive. Proxies with permission."
- **Sound:** A single soft musical sting under the VO.
