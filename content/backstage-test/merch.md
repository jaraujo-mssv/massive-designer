## Photo style

A bright merch lookbook photo against a seamless light warm-grey studio backdrop. Full-frame camera, 85mm lens at f/4: the face and the print on their top sharp, the backdrop softly out of focus. A large soft key light from the front, gentle fill, a faint rim light separating them from the backdrop. Relaxed and confident, looking into the lens, as if for a brand's shop page. True-to-life skin tones and colours, the top's white and orange accurate, a clean grade with fine grain. Photorealistic: a real person, photographed for real.
