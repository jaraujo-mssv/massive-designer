## Sets

### The gym

A no-shortcuts strength gym: raw concrete walls, black rubber floor, chalk dust hanging in the light, steel racks, a single warm coral light strip along the back wall. His worn leather training log titled "Labors" sits on a bench.

## Shots

### Shot 1 · 6s

- **Visual:** Gym. Hercules deadlifts a barbell loaded with car tires.
- **Camera:** Wide, low angle, static, then a slow push-in to medium as he locks out.
- **Action:** Hercules grips a barbell loaded with car tires, deadlifts it to lockout in one smooth pull, holds it, and grins at the camera.
- **Line:** VO: "The strongest man in history just signed with Massive." Hercules: "Labor 13. Let's go."
- **Sound:** Rubber tires creaking, the bar flexing, a heavy exhale, gym room tone.

### Shot 2 · 6s

- **Visual:** On his phone, he digs through an app's settings for a way to stop sharing. Only option: "Email support." He strains.
- **Camera:** Over the shoulder, close on the phone in his huge hand, then a close-up of his face, eye level.
- **Action:** His thumb scrolls deeper and deeper through settings menus. He reaches the bottom: the only option is "Email support." He strains, jaw clenched, a vein rising on his forehead.
- **Screen:** An app's settings, menus nested inside menus. The only option at the bottom reads "Email support."
- **Line:** "I held up the sky. I can't find a pause button."
- **Sound:** Rapid screen taps, a strained grunt.

### Shot 3 · 4s

- **Visual:** A Massive-powered app: one tap on the sharing switch. On-screen: "Off. Sharing stops now."
- **Camera:** Insert, top-down close-up on the phone screen in his hand.
- **Action:** His thumb taps the sharing switch once; it slides to off.
- **Screen:** A Massive-powered app with a single sharing switch. After the tap it reads "Off. Sharing stops now."
- **Line:** "One tap. Done."
- **Sound:** One soft click.

### Shot 4 · 8s

- **Visual:** Old app: he uninstalls it, the icon pops back, then two more, like Hydra heads. Cut to the Massive app: uninstall, "0 apps found."
- **Camera:** Insert on the home screen, cut to a medium shot of Hercules glaring at the phone, then back to the insert.
- **Action:** He drags the old app's icon to uninstall. It pops back, then two more copies sprout beside it like Hydra heads. He glares. Cut: he uninstalls the Massive app, and the search shows "0 apps found." He shrugs.
- **Screen:** A home screen where one app icon multiplies into three. Then a search result reading "0 apps found."
- **Line:** "Easier than the Hydra."
- **Sound:** Three quick pops as the icons reappear, then one clean chime.

### Shot 5 · 6s

- **Visual:** End card, Hercules flexing.
- **Camera:** Medium, low angle, static, then the graphic end card.
- **Action:** Hercules flexes both arms, holds it, then the end card takes over.
- **Line:** VO: "He earned it the hard way. So do we. Earned trust beats paid fame. Massive. Proxies with permission."
- **Sound:** A single soft musical sting under the VO.
