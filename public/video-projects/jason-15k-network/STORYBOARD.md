Followers as nodes, in Massive's network language. The dots are drawn on one canvas from precomputed targets in
`js/points.js` (a halftone of `assets/template/jason.jpg` and the "15K" glyphs in Outfit 900). The copy never claims
the dot count is 15,000; the counter shows the share of dots that have landed, scaled to 15,000.
