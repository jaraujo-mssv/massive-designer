A test set for the photo generation pipeline: the same visit day as the Legends backstage photos, but the cast is Jason Grad hosting three real celebrities, The Rock, John Cena and Michael Jordan. The settings match the Legends set so the results can be compared. Internal only, not for publishing.

Each item has a prompt for image generation. "Copy prompt" in the Content tab adds everyone's descriptions, the photo style below and the reference images to attach.

## Photo style

Candid documentary photography of a celebrity ambassador's visit day, like a high-end press kit shot by an editorial photographer. Full-frame camera, 35mm to 85mm lenses at wide apertures: shallow depth of field, often framed past out-of-focus foreground elements such as a shoulder, the back of a head, a microphone or a camera rig. Natural available light mixed with soft practical light, true-to-life skin tones, a slightly warm and gently desaturated grade with soft contrast and fine grain. Real moments, never poses: laughing mid-sentence, glancing off camera, people around them reacting. Slightly imperfect reportage framing. Photorealistic: everyone is a real person in a real place.

Indoors: dark charcoal interiors with warm coral accents and light oak floors.
