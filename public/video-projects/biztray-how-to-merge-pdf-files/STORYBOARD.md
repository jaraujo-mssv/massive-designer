How to merge PDF files on your own Windows PC, without uploading a contract to a server you have never heard of.

The first search result wants all four documents on its servers, free up to a limit and watermarked on the way out. None of that is necessary. When you combine pdf files the pages are copied as they are, so nothing is re-numbered: four documents that each started at page one now count to four and start again. Stamping a fresh sequence is the usual second step.
