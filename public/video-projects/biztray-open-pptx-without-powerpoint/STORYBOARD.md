---

To open a PPTX file without PowerPoint, decide first whether you are presenting the deck or reading it once.

For reading, a deck is a stack of pages, and converting powerpoint to pdf gives you something every machine opens and nobody edits by accident. For presenting or editing, LibreOffice Impress is free and offline. The catch with a PDF is that everything which made it a presentation is gone: animations, transitions, embedded video and the speaker notes.
