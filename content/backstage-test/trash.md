Failed generations, kept for the laughs. Nothing here is used anywhere. Internal only, not for publishing.

This list isn't shown in the Content tab: its images show, in this order, on the `/trash` page (no nav link,
only by URL). The images are in `public/content/backstage-test/photos/trash/`, all WebP at 1080p or under. To
trash an image, put it there in any format and add its item here with `Image: trash/<file>` (a take from
photos.md loses its `Prompt:`, so `npm run content:photos` never regenerates it), then run
`npm run content:trash`: it turns anything that isn't WebP yet, or is bigger than 1920×1080, into a 1080p WebP and
updates its `Image:` line.
