Production portraits of the three guests against a dark navy backdrop: one vertical photo each, framed from the head to the waist, in their suit, with a Massive icon pin on the lapel and a serious expression. Unlike the backstage photos, these are posed and lit, like a campaign's official portraits. Internal only, not for publishing.

Each item has a prompt for image generation. "Copy prompt" in the Content tab adds their description, the photo style below and the reference images to attach.

## Photo style

A polished production portrait for a celebrity ambassador campaign, shot by the production's stills photographer against a seamless dark navy backdrop with a soft, slightly lighter glow behind the shoulders. Full-frame camera, 85mm lens at f/2.8: the face and the lapel pin tack sharp. A deliberate, posed portrait, square to the camera, looking into the lens with a serious, composed expression: no smile. Campaign lighting: a large soft key light from the front left, gentle fill, and a warm coral rim light separating them from the navy. True-to-life skin tones, rich but soft contrast, a clean grade with fine grain. Photorealistic: a real person, photographed for real.
