## Cast

### Designer

A product designer in their late twenties, black oversized sweater, round tortoiseshell glasses, a lanyard, slightly nervous, carrying an open silver laptop.

## Sets

### Washington's office

A modern product office in the evening: dark charcoal walls, a long walnut desk, a warm coral desk lamp, floor-to-ceiling windows with soft city lights behind. On the wall behind the desk hangs a framed copy of the US Constitution, his signature at the top.

## Shots

### Shot 1 · 6s

- **Visual:** Modern office, a framed copy of the Constitution on the wall, his signature at the top.
- **Camera:** Wide to medium, eye level. Slow push-in from the doorway toward the desk, tilting down from the framed Constitution to Washington.
- **Action:** Washington signs a document at his desk with a fountain pen, caps the pen, and glances up at the frame.
- **Line:** VO: "George Washington signed the Constitution first. It still needed nine states to say yes."
- **Sound:** Pen scratching on paper, quiet office room tone.

### Shot 2 · 6s

- **Visual:** A designer shows him an install screen: consent buried in the terms, one box already ticked. He leans in.
- **Camera:** Over the designer's shoulder onto the laptop screen, then a medium close-up of Washington leaning in, eye level.
- **Action:** The designer sets the open laptop on the desk and turns it toward him. Washington leans in, eyes narrowing at the ticked box, then looks up at the designer.
- **Screen:** An app install screen. The consent wording sits in small print inside the terms, and one checkbox is already ticked.
- **Line:** "Is that a pre-ticked box?" Designer: "It's... a design choice."
- **Sound:** Laptop set down on wood, a short awkward pause.

### Shot 3 · 4s

- **Visual:** He slides the laptop back and nods at the frame on the wall.
- **Camera:** Medium, static, slightly low angle, the framed Constitution visible over his shoulder.
- **Action:** He slides the laptop back across the desk with two fingers, then tilts his head toward the frame behind him without breaking eye contact.
- **Line:** "Even the Constitution needed a yes."
- **Sound:** Laptop sliding across the desk.

### Shot 4 · 4s

- **Visual:** The fixed screen: its own page, Massive named, plain language, empty box, Decline as big as Accept.
- **Camera:** Insert, close on the laptop screen, slow push-in.
- **Action:** The cursor drifts between Decline and Accept, which are the same size; the checkbox stays empty. Washington is heard off screen.
- **Screen:** A consent page on its own screen. The heading names Massive. Short plain-language text. An empty checkbox. Decline and Accept buttons the same size.
- **Line:** "Say who you are. Say what you use."
- **Sound:** Quiet room tone, a soft trackpad click.

### Shot 5 · 6s

- **Visual:** The designer looks at the empty box, unsure.
- **Camera:** Two-shot, static, eye level. The designer soft in the foreground, Washington sharp behind the desk.
- **Action:** The designer stares at the empty checkbox, then at Washington. Washington leans back in his chair and folds his arms, completely calm.
- **Line:** Designer: "So we just... wait for them to agree?" Washington: "Nothing takes effect until it's ratified."
- **Sound:** Chair creaks as he leans back.

### Shot 6 · 6s

- **Visual:** End card.
- **Camera:** Graphic end card.
- **Line:** VO: "He earned it by asking for a yes. So do we. Earned trust beats paid fame. Massive. Proxies with permission."
- **Sound:** A single soft musical sting under the VO.
