# SFX credits

`pop.mp3`, `click-soft.mp3`, `ping.mp3`, `sparkle.mp3` and `chime.mp3`
come from HyperFrames' bundled media-use library, sourced from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/)
under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): free for commercial
and non-commercial use, no attribution required.

`slack-knock.mp3` is Slack's notification knock, supplied by the team. It plays once, as the banner lands
(mixed at 3.05 s with its 0.28 s lead-in trimmed).

The bed in `../` (`sfx-*.mp3`) is mixed from these files by `npm run video:sfx holiday-greetings`, following the cue sheet
next to it (`sfx-*.cues.json`). Edit the sheet and re-run to move a cue; no other sounds are used.
